How To Measure

Easy Size & Measurement Guide

For custom orders and perfect sizing, we just need 4 simple body measurements.

A

1. Overbust & Full Bust

Measure horizontally across the fullest point of the bust while wearing an unpadded supportive bralette.

B

2. Underbust Line

Directly beneath the breast tissue where the rib cage begins. Tape must be snug and horizontal.

C

3. Natural Waist

The narrowest contour of your torso (typically 1-2 inches above the navel). Do not suck in.

D

4. High Hip & Illiac Crest

Across the top curve of the pelvic hip bones where the corset base terminates.
