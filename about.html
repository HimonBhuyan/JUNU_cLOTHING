The Manifesto

"We don't merely tailor cloth; we construct an armor of quiet defiance."

Born from a desire to escape the homogeneity of fast-fashion boutiques, JUNU emerged in Kathmandu as a dedicated couture laboratory. Every garment begins not on a digital screen, but draped directly by hand over wooden tailoring busts.

By pairing ancestral European corsetry techniques—such as herringbone French cotton coutil and high-tensile German spiral steel stays—with raw Himalayan textures, liquid lamé pleats, and artisanal beadwork, JUNU crafts personal wearable sculptures that command reverence.

Our pieces are never mass-manufactured. Each release is strictly limited to micro-batches (often between 5 and 18 numbered units), ensuring each wearer owns an authentic, unreplicated artifact of couture art.

The Anatomy of Architecture

Commercial fast fashion shapewear relies on flimsy plastic boning that bends, snaps, and digs painfully into the ribcage. At JUNU, we reject shortcuts. Every corseted creation utilizes multi-layered German spiral steel stays encased in plush velvet tunnels.

The spiral structure flexes dynamically with your natural breathing and body movements while maintaining an unyielding hourglass architectural column.

Couturier Standards

3D Anatomical Measurement Protocol

For custom made-to-measure orders and bespoke steel boning commissions, we require 4 precise anatomical circumference points.

A

1. Overbust & Full Bust

Measure horizontally across the fullest point of the bust while wearing an unpadded supportive bralette.

B

2. Underbust Ribcage

Directly beneath the breast tissue along the lower ribcage line.

C

3. Natural Waist

The narrowest indentation of the torso, approximately 2 inches above the navel.

D

4. High Hip & Illiac Crest

Across the top curve of the pelvic hip bones where the corset base terminates.
