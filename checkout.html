Couturier Standards

3D Anatomical Measurement Protocol

For custom made-to-measure orders and bespoke steel boning commissions, we require 4 precise anatomical circumference points.

A

1. Overbust & Full Bust

Measure horizontally across the fullest point of the bust while wearing an unpadded supportive bralette.

B

2. Underbust Ribcage

Directly beneath the breast tissue along the lower ribcage line.

C

3. Natural Waist

The narrowest indentation of the torso, approximately 2 inches above the navel.

D

4. High Hip & Illiac Crest

Across the top curve of the pelvic hip bones where the corset base terminates.
